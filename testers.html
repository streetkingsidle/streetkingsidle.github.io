Become a tester

Street Kings: Idle Tycoon · Android · free

Street Kings is a cartoon idle tycoon game coming to Google Play. Before it launches, Google requires a test with real players. If you have an Android phone and a Gmail account, you can help, and you will get to play it before everyone else.

What we are asking

  • Join the test and keep the game installed for 14 days.
  • Open it now and then. A couple of minutes a few times a week is plenty.
  • Tell us about anything that looks broken. Feedback is welcome but not required.

In this test version, ads and purchases are test versions only. Nothing real is charged.

How to join

  1. Send us your Gmail address. Email it to support@stafbyte.com with the subject "Street Kings tester". We add you to the tester list. It must be the Google account you use on your Android phone.
  2. Open the opt-in link. We will reply with the link. Open it on your phone while signed in to that Google account, and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install from Google Play. Tap the Play Store link on that page to install the game.
  4. Keep it installed for 14 days and open it occasionally. That's all.
Email your Gmail to join Join the Discord

Swapping tests? Tell us the name of your app in the email and we will test yours too.

Your privacy

We only use your Gmail address to add you to the test list. We do not share or sell it. The game itself has no accounts and keeps its saves on your device. See the privacy policy.
