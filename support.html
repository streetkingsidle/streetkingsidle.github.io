Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. There are no accounts. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, the progress is gone and we cannot restore it. Use the export option in Settings to keep a backup.

I bought something and did not receive it

Open the game and wait a moment, then check the Shop. Purchases are handled by Google Play, so also check your Play purchase history. If it is still missing, email us with your Google Play order number.

How do refunds work?

Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy. You can request one from your Play purchase history.

What does a time skip do?

It credits your automated earnings as if the chosen number of hours had passed. It does not move timers or events.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Ads are always optional and the game is fully playable without them.

How do I delete my data?

All game data is stored on your device. Clear the game's storage in Android settings or uninstall the game and it is deleted. See the privacy policy for what ad and purchase providers collect.

Where can I report a bug?

Email us. Screenshots help a lot.
