Privacy Policy

Street Kings: Idle Tycoon · Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what the game "Street Kings: Idle Tycoon" ("the game", "we") does with information. We wrote it to be short and plain.

Developer: Stafbyte
Contact: support@stafbyte.com

The short version

  • The game has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. We do not run a server that stores your game.
  • The game shows optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch them) and sells optional in-app purchases. The companies that provide those services collect some information, described below.
  • The game is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

What stays on your device

Your progress (cash, crew, cards, chapters, settings, and the name and avatar you pick for your profile) is stored in the game's local storage on your device. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, that progress is deleted. We cannot see it or restore it. The profile name is only shown inside the game; we do not send it anywhere.

Ads

The game offers rewarded ads: you tap a button to watch a short video and get a reward (for example, double income for a while). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads and measure how they perform, the ad provider may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your approximate location (from your IP address),
  • device and app information (model, operating system, app version),
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or watched).

Ad providers may use this to show ads that are more relevant to you, subject to their own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

You can control ad personalisation in your device's settings (on Android: Settings > Google > Ads, where you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads). You are never required to watch an ad to play.

In-app purchases

You can buy optional items (Stars, card packs and time skips). Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made so the game can deliver the item. Purchases are handled under Google Play's terms and refund policy.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, precise location or any health data. We do not sell personal information.

Analytics and crash reports

The game does not include analytics or crash reporting of its own.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given personal information to an ad or purchase provider through the game, contact us at the address above.

Your choices

  • Do not watch ads: the game stays fully playable.
  • Reset or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings.
  • Delete your progress at any time by clearing the game's data or uninstalling it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
